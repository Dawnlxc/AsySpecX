# AsySpecX Phase 11 Stage B: TS-Specific Spectral/Complex Kernel

## Verdict

Stage B passes on the frozen Weather `L=96, H=720, cut_freq=13` cell.
The resulting operator is a compact **asymmetric past-to-future temporal
kernel**, not a variable-to-variable lead--lag kernel:

1. a rank-8, channel-separable past-to-future map;
2. a positive real spectral-mixture envelope on each temporal analysis mode;
3. an optional bounded within-variable analytic phase rotation.

The predeclared validation-first winner is `fk_sm4_ph4_h`. Against its real-SM
anchor it improves mean validation MSE, wins all 3 paired test seeds, adds only
32 parameters, and stays inside the complex-stage fixed-work resource gate.
The test gain is nevertheless only `0.00002624` (`0.00768%`), so the honest
claim is **a replicated marginal refinement**, not a large forecasting gain.

Across the complete Stage-A-to-Stage-B path, the selected complex kernel has
mean test MSE `0.3416851`, versus `0.3418097` for Stage A (`-0.03647%`) and
`0.3425529` for dense direct (`-0.25335%`). It uses 76,018 parameters, 45.04%
fewer than dense direct. It still does not beat the published Weather H720
reference `0.3387`; the gap is `0.0029851` (`0.8813%`).

Cross-variable mixing was never implemented. The complex component rotates
only the in-phase and quadrature latents of the **same variable**, so this
result gives no support to multivariate lead--lag modeling.

## Kernel

### Stage-A temporal asymmetry

For every channel independently,

\[
\widehat Y_{b,h,c}=\sum_r U_{h,r}s_{c,r}
\langle X_{b,:,c},V_r\rangle.
\]

`U` is horizon-conditioned, `V` analyzes the observed past, and `s[c,r]`
changes the amplitude of a shared temporal mode without mixing channel values.
This is asymmetric because the past and future have different bases and roles.

### Real spectral-mixture envelope

For temporal mode `r`, Stage B applies

\[
V_r^{SM}=V_r+\operatorname{irfft}\left[
(A_r(\omega)-1)\operatorname{rfft}(V_r)\right].
\]

`A_r(omega)` is a positive Gaussian-mixture envelope with geometric mean one.
Its signed log-gain is bounded and zero-initialized. Therefore:

- zero gain is an exact Stage-A identity;
- the real-SM stage changes spectral amplitude but not Fourier phase;
- the response is shared across channels and cannot express lead--lag;
- 2- and 4-component mode-specific envelopes add only 56 and 104 parameters.

### Bounded within-variable complex phase

The complex ablation forms an analytic latent for each channel and mode:

\[
z^R_{bcr}=\langle X_{bc},V_r^{SM}\rangle,\qquad
z^Q_{bcr}=\langle X_{bc},\mathcal H(V_r^{SM})\rangle,
\]

then decodes with

\[
\widehat Y_{bhc}=\sum_r U_{hr}s_{cr}
\left(\cos\phi_{hr}z^R_{bcr}+\sin\phi_{hr}z^Q_{bcr}\right).
\]

`phi[h,r]` is generated by 2 or 4 fixed cosine horizon bases and a learned
rank-by-basis coefficient table. It is bounded by `tanh` and initialized to
zero, giving exact real-SM identity at initialization. The output stays real.
The quadrature is the cyclic Hilbert transform of the learned past basis; it
does not inspect future samples.

## Frozen execution protocol

- Dataset/cell: Weather, `seq_len=96`, `pred_len=720`, `cut_freq=13`.
- Existing individual spectral lift and full cycle residual were frozen.
- All training jobs requested one H100; seeds were 2024, 2025, and 2026.
- Canary: two epochs, finite loss/diagnostics, active non-collapsed gates.
- Screen: seed 2026, validation only, `defer_test=1`.
- Promotion: strict validation gain, fewer parameters than dense direct,
  active finite diagnostics, and fixed-work train/inference ratios at most
  `1.10x` relative to the stage anchor.
- Test was opened only after validation promotion.
- Stable win: lower three-seed mean validation MSE and at least 2/3 paired
  test-MSE wins over the stage anchor.
- Complex phase was implemented and run only after real SM passed its stable
  three-seed gate.

Both mode-specific real-SM arms pass the stable rule.

| `fk_sm2_mode` | 0.6852830 | **0.3416446 +/- 0.0001653** | 3/3 | **0.3383220** | **75,938** | yes |

`fk_sm4_mode` was selected as the complex-stage anchor because its replicated
mean validation MSE is lower. `fk_sm2_mode` has the best observed test MSE in
the entire Stage-B matrix, but switching to it after opening test would violate
the selection protocol. It remains the strongest cost-sensitive Pareto arm,
not the declared validation-selected winner.

The shared-envelope arm and frozen-base control were rejected at validation:
the former did not beat Stage A and the latter lost `0.0199%` validation MSE.
This supports mode-specific spectral shaping on top of a trainable past basis,
not replacement of that basis by a fixed DCT plus envelope.

## Resource audit

Fixed-work measurements used synthetic tensors, batch size 64, 20 train
forward/backward repetitions, 50 inference repetitions, and one NVIDIA H100.

| Arm | Comparison anchor | Parameters | Train ratio | Inference ratio | Peak-memory ratio |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `fk_sm2_mode` | Stage A | 75,938 | 1.094x | 1.025x | 1.0003x |
| `fk_sm4_mode` | Stage A | 75,986 | 1.097x | 1.026x | 1.0004x |
| `fk_sm4_ph2_q` | real `sm4` | 76,002 | 1.048x | 1.051x | 1.0055x |
| `fk_sm4_ph4_h` | real `sm4` | 76,018 | 1.044x | 1.062x | 1.0056x |

For the final arm, fixed-work peak CUDA allocation is 130.47 MiB versus
129.75 MiB for real SM and 134.70 MiB for dense direct. Composing the two
stage-local ratios gives approximately `1.146x` training, `1.089x` inference,
and `1.006x` peak memory versus Stage A. Thus complex phase is cheap in
parameters and memory but not free in compute.

## Decision

1. Retain `fk_sm4_ph4_h` as the formal Stage-B winner because it passes every
   frozen gate and has the best replicated validation result among eligible
   arms.
2. Retain `fk_sm2_mode` as the practical low-overhead Pareto alternative. Its
   lower observed test MSE is reported but is not used post hoc to overturn
   the validation-selected winner.
3. Prune shared SM, frozen-base SM, and the `ph4_q` phase arm.
4. Keep cross-variable lead--lag out of this kernel family. The evidence here
   concerns only temporal forecastability within each variable.
5. Do not claim a general TS kernel win yet. The next defensible gate is a
   locked multi-dataset/multi-horizon replication comparing only Stage A,
   `sm2_mode`, and `sm4_ph4_h`; the current result proves viability on one
   hard Weather H720 cell, not universality.
